Privacy and data handling

Coastal Contract Watch is a product of Harbour Line Advisory. This draft outlines our planned treatment of enquiries and subscriber information and must be approved before live customer acquisition or payment collection.

Information used

Enquiries can contain business contact details, company name, preferred sectors and regions. When subscriptions are activated, the payment provider will process billing details under its own terms.

Purpose

We plan to use information to respond to enquiries, deliver subscribed research and manage legitimate customer-service obligations. Separate marketing messages require appropriate consent.

Retention and rights

We will publish final retention periods, service-provider details, access controls, correction processes and privacy-contact arrangements before launch. For requests now, contact info@harbourlineadvisory.com.

This draft cannot be treated as a final privacy notice and should not be published as operative legal terms without review.
