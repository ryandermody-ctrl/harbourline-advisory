Purpose and accountability · Policy under development

We want half the profit to do some good.

Our proposal is simple. If this becomes a profitable business, we would allocate 50% of distributable profit to eligible charities. We still need to approve the accounting policy and choose recipients. No donations have been made.

Pre-launch disclosure: This is a proposed giving policy, not evidence of an established charitable partnership or donations already made. No beneficiary has been selected. There are no current product donation figures.

How we would calculate it

01 / COLLECT

Start with actual sales

Report earned subscription revenue net of refunds and sales taxes collected for remittance.

02 / ACCOUNT

Deduct the costs of running it

Deduct genuine and documented service expenses, allocated overhead and required tax reserves according to an agreed written accounting policy.

03 / GIVE

Report what we actually gave

Allocate 50% of the remaining distributable profit to eligible charities. Publish the amount, payment date, recipient and receipt confirmation after settlement.

Where the money could go

We are considering marine rescue, maritime training, ocean conservation and veterans’ charities. We will name recipients only after confirming their eligibility and approving them.

Safeguards

  • All charitable transfers must come from Harbour Line Advisory through approved banking authorities. We never transfer customer subscription funds directly to third parties without a clear contractual basis.
  • Only qualified donees verified through the CRA will be presented as eligible for corporate charitable donation treatment.
  • Payments for a commercial subscription are purchases of a service. Subscribers do not receive charitable donation receipts for their subscription fees.
  • We will not claim any donation or affiliation until the transfer and recipient are independently confirmed.
  • If there is no distributable profit, the calculated donation is zero, and that will be reported transparently.

Reporting

Following launch, the intended reporting frequency is quarterly. The public report will show revenue, approved costs, distributable profit, 50% donation obligation, cumulative payments and any amount outstanding. Commercially sensitive details may be summarized but every claimed donation must be supported by payment evidence and an eligible recipient.

This policy is a pre-launch draft. Final commitments, definitions and charitable donation treatment require approval by Harbour Line Advisory’s authorized management and accounting adviser. Corporate donations may qualify for tax treatment subject to CRA rules; no subscriber tax benefit is implied. See the CRA charity register for recipient verification.
