THE POINT OF THE WEEKLY EMAIL

A tender alert is the easy part. Is the work worth pricing?

A tug operator needs to know more than that a notice mentions tugboats. We want to say which requirements matter, whether there is a realistic route to the job, and what still needs checking in the tender package.

We cannot promise to find every notice or tell you whether you qualify. This is a pilot. We want contractors to tell us where it falls short.
