FIRST / SIX QUESTIONS

What do we know for certain?

Answer based on what you have checked today. If a mandatory requirement cannot be met, the result will show Stop.

OPTIONAL / CHECK SOME WORDING

Is there a clause you want a second look at?

Paste a section of a public tender. The browser will look for wording relating to bonds, insurance, manning, vessel class, delay penalties and other common issues. It can miss clauses and cannot interpret the full contract.

Your text is evaluated in this browser and is not sent to Harbour Line Advisory. Shared links contain no tender text.

FINALLY / YOUR ESTIMATING COST

How much will it cost to put the bid together?

Enter the hours, loaded labour rate and any outside costs. These are your estimates.

Include management time, travel, consultants and bonding fees if they apply.
