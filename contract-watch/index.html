BEFORE THE ESTIMATOR STARTS

Can we qualify? Can we do the job? Will it make money?

01 / CAN WE?

Can we prove we qualify?

Check the pass/fail criteria against the actual certificates, crew qualifications and past-project evidence.

02 / CAN WE DELIVER?

Have we got the vessel and crew?

Check the named vessel, where it is now, who can crew it, and how it reaches the job.

03 / SHOULD WE?

Have we priced the whole job?

Add the tow, fuel, crew changes, weather standby, insurance and time spent waiting to be paid.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

What does the clause checker actually do?

It looks for wording associated with 21 common risk topics. It can miss things or flag something harmless. Read the original clause and get specialist advice where needed.

What goes into a shared result?

The shared result contains a rating and issue counts, without your tender text or pricing. Only paste material you are allowed to process.

Is the paid brief available?

Not yet. We are testing the service and have disabled checkout.

Where do the tenders come from?

From public procurement notices. We are independent of the agencies issuing them. Always check the official notice and amendments.
